What is a Financial Scam?

A financial scam is an attempt to trick people into giving away money, banking information, payment credentials or other sensitive financial information.

Common Financial Scams

  • Fake investment opportunities
  • Fake customer support calls
  • Fraudulent payment requests
  • Fake lottery or prize messages
  • Job-related payment scams
  • Online shopping fraud

🚨 Warning Signs

⚠️ Someone asks you to transfer money urgently.

⚠️ Someone asks for your OTP or PIN.

⚠️ An investment promises guaranteed high returns.

⚠️ You are asked to pay a fee to receive an unexpected prize.

How to Stay Safe

  • Verify the person or organization independently.
  • Do not share banking credentials.
  • Do not trust guaranteed-profit claims.
  • Check transaction details before confirming payments.
  • Report suspicious financial activity quickly.
