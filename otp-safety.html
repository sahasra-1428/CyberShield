What is an OTP?

OTP stands for One-Time Password. It is a temporary verification code used to confirm certain online actions or logins.

🚨 Never Share Your OTP

Never share an OTP with another person, even if they claim to be from a bank, company, customer-support team or another trusted organization.

Common OTP Scams

  • Fake customer support calls
  • Fake banking messages
  • Account verification scams
  • Prize or lottery scams
  • Fake delivery-related messages

🛡️ OTP Safety Tips

✔ Keep OTPs confidential.

✔ Read the message before entering the code.

✔ Do not enter an OTP on suspicious websites.

✔ If you receive an unexpected OTP, investigate your account.

✔ Contact the organization through its official channels if needed.
