Why Social Media Safety Matters

Social media accounts contain personal information, photos, conversations and connections. Protecting this information reduces the risk of scams and account misuse.

Common Risks

  • Fake profiles
  • Suspicious links
  • Account impersonation
  • Privacy leaks
  • Online scams
  • Malicious messages

🛡️ Safety Tips

✔ Keep your account privacy settings appropriate.

✔ Accept requests only from people you know.

✔ Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

✔ Do not click suspicious links.

✔ Use a strong and unique password.

✔ Enable multi-factor authentication.

✔ Report fake or suspicious accounts.
