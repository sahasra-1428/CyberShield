What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers pretend to be trusted individuals or organizations to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

Common Signs of Phishing

  • Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
  • Suspicious or unfamiliar URLs.
  • Urgent messages demanding immediate action.
  • Spelling and grammatical mistakes.
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs or banking information.
  • Offers that seem too good to be true.

How to Stay Safe

  • Check the sender before responding.
  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Verify website addresses carefully.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious messages.
⚠️ Remember: A trusted company will not normally ask you to reveal your password or OTP through an unsolicited message.
