🆘 Cyber Crime Help

Follow these steps if you think you have encountered an online scam.

🔗

Suspicious Link

Do not click unknown links. Verify the website address before entering passwords or personal details.

📱

Scam Call

Do not share OTPs, passwords, PINs or banking information with unknown callers.

💬

Suspicious Message

Avoid clicking links or downloading files from unexpected messages. Verify the sender first.

🔐

Account Security

If you think your account is compromised, change your password and enable additional security features.

💳

Financial Scam

If money or banking information is involved, contact your bank through its official contact channels.

🚨

Report Incident

Use the CyberShield report page to submit details of a suspicious cyber incident.
