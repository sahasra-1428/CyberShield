🚨 Security Alerts

Stay informed about common cyber threats and suspicious online activities.

⚠️
HIGH RISK

Phishing Links

Be careful when clicking unknown links received through SMS, email, WhatsApp or social media.

Safety Tip: Verify the website address before entering your information.
🔐
MEDIUM RISK

OTP Scams

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank or government organization.

Safety Tip: Keep your OTP and banking credentials private.
📱
MEDIUM RISK

Fake Customer Support

Scammers may pretend to be customer-care representatives and ask for personal or financial information.

Safety Tip: Use only official websites or applications for customer support.
🎁
WARNING

Fake Prize Messages

Messages claiming that you won a prize may be attempts to steal your personal or financial information.

Safety Tip: Do not click unknown links or pay money to claim unexpected prizes.
🛡️ Stay Alert. Stay Safe.
